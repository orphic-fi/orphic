how a loss moves

senior loses $1 in a market

Senior's share price drops at once. At the next weekly settle junior sends $1 of its idle USDC into the senior vault, and junior's share price takes the hit. Morpho's vault then recognises the payment at a capped pace (at most 0.55% of the vault a day): a 2% cover takes about four days to show fully in senior's price. Senior holders who stay are made whole; anyone who withdraws inside that window realises the part not yet recognised. The app shows "cover in progress" while this is happening. Week one brings an hourly cover call that removes the wait for the weekly settle.

junior is gone

Senior is impaired only after junior's whole $75,000 is gone: a 15% loss across the entire stack inside one week. Nothing in senior's venues has done that; the scenario that would is a stablecoin failure, which hits every USDC lender the same way, without a buffer.

fees

Every fee Orphic earns lands in one router and is split 50% senior / 25% junior / 25% treasury. Senior's share is paid into the vault, which raises its share price with no fee switch. Streams at launch: the ORPH pool's creator fee. Later: agent token fees, agent weekly fee streams, creation fees and tuition (those two are 50% burned, 50% treasury).
