### hyperbeat-earn USDC (10.40% on $3.83M)
- What it is: the DefiLlama pool is Hyperbeat's new USD+ (masterUSD) vault, contract 0x9065E3153B1393Bb5f76520cdc1e08E49eb04B03, launched 2026-11-20; the adapter was merged 2026-11-23, so this is a brand new listing, not a venue that just crossed the bar. The legacy hbUSDC vault is retired and winding down. Hyperbeat is a Hyperliquid-native suite (beHYPE liquid staking with ether.fi, Earn vaults, Morphobeat credit, Liquid Banking card and beatUSD).
- Who runs it: "Hyperbeat Labs", no named individuals in docs. $5.2M seed (Aug 2025) co-led by ether.fi Ventures and Electric Capital, with Coinbase Ventures, Chapter One, Selini, Maelstrom, Anchorage Digital.
- Where the yield comes from: stablecoin lending across HyperLend, Morpho markets (Gauntlet, Felix, kHYPE/WHYPE/UBTC collateral), beatUSD "Liquid Bank credit" Morpho markets and HyperCore Earn USDC; a KuCoin item also mentions delta-neutral strategies. No HLP. The 10.40% headline is a 30-day exchange-rate read on a four-day-old vault; the adapter PR itself showed about 7.4%. Treat as unseasoned.
- Audits: Codespect on the Morpho orchestrator (Dec 2025, 1 medium fixed) and withdrawal queue (Jan 2026, 1 medium fixed); Nethermind on beHYPE/vaults (1 critical fixed pre-deploy). CertiK lists no audit or bounty. No exploit found. Hindenrank grades C+ and flags downstream-protocol cascade risk.
- TVL trend: Hyperbeat Earn as a whole fell from about $477M (Sep 2025) to $188M (Nov 2025), $70M (Jan 2026), $34M (Apr), $20M (Oct), about $16.5M now, down 15.6% in 30 days. Long steady outflow.
- Exit terms: queued withdrawals only, typically about 10 minutes, no stated fee, shares locked until processed, Hyperbeat can reject requests. Performance fee on USD+ not found (legacy hbUSDC took 20%).
- Admin risk: MultiRolesAuthority roles (ALLOCATOR, KEEPER sets exchange rate, SOLVER processes withdrawals) behind an upgradeable proxy; docs name no multisig, timelock or signers. Unverified: multisig setup, fees, founders.
- Note: much of this is lending into Morpho and HyperLend markets Orphic already touches directly, so the extra 6 points over Felix is mostly leverage, beatUSD credit exposure and a new wrapper layer.
