How to read it. **Order: holder exit request (by the deadline) → S1 → A and B (only after S1) → C (B + 3 days) → D → S2 → claim and redeposit right after S2 → v2's own first settle.** v2's constructor sets `lastSettle = block.timestamp`, so v2's first settle is due `A + 601,200 s`; the redeposit right after S2 must land **before** it, so v2 is funded when it first settles. For A = B at Mon 16 Nov 00:00 UTC the margin over S2 is **+3.55 h**; at Mon 16 Nov 12:00 UTC **+15.55 h** (`A = S1` itself has margin 0 and fails). The holder deadline is derived from the contract's own `requestExit` formula (`readyAt = (t + 7 d + 1 d - 1) / 1 d * 1 d`, first settle at or after it): a request at **Sun 15 Nov 00:00:00 UTC** has readyAt Sun 22 Nov 00:00 and is priced at S2; one second later is not. Ask the holder to request by **Sat 14 Nov 23:59:59 UTC**.

**Direction of the rule.** A later A only widens the margin (v2's first settle moves later); what limits A from above is that C (B + 3 d) and D must be done before S2 so v2 is open for the redeposit: the latest A = B is **Thu 19 Nov 19:27 UTC**. A before S1 is refused by the scripts.

### If a date slips

Every later date is a function of the earlier ones: re-run the calendar script (it reads the chain) and use these rules.
- **S1 is late** (keeper late, up to the watchdog's 4 h): S2 = actual S1 + 6 d 23 h moves with it; A and B stay "on/after S1" (the plans refuse earlier).
- **A (= B) later than planned**, e.g. **Tue 17 Nov 00:00**: C earliest Fri 20 Nov 00:00, v2's first settle due Mon 23 Nov 23:00, margin over S2 **+27.55 h**; still inside the window. Wed 18 Nov 00:00: C Sat 21 Nov, margin +51.55 h. Thu 19 Nov 12:00: C Sun 22 Nov 12:00, margin +87.55 h, the last day that works. **Fri 20 Nov or later: C and D land after S2**, so v2 is not open at S2: either hold the claim (the old junior keeps its capital, nothing is lost, the claim is simply later) or accept an unfunded v2 gap (see M1 below). Do not skip the old settle to fit.
- **B later than A** (the signer is not ready): C moves with B; the table's row for B is the same as A's as long as B + 3 d <= S2.
- **The holder misses the deadline** (a request after Sun 15 Nov 00:00:00): it is priced at the settle after S2 (about 29 Nov) and the redeposit moves with it; the old junior stays the cover until then.

## Who covers senior while both juniors exist (corrected)

Cover is not tied to the fee recipient. Whichever junior settles after a loss pays senior's shortfall from its own idle USDC.

- **Not "sequential, never twice" in general.** The second junior to settle sees a repaired price only if the first paid in full. A junior that pays only part books the rest as a carry (`carryPerShare`) and bills it again at its next settle, even if the other junior repaired senior in between; that is a double payment, bounded by the paying junior's idle USDC, going to senior holders. The old junior is a pre-fix build with its own bookkeeping.
- **The dangerous case is an unfunded v2 settling.** If v2 holds only its 1 USDC seed when its first settle comes after a loss L: it pays about 1 USDC and books carry L-1; the old junior's S2 then repairs senior from its idle; v2's carry is still billed later (double pay); the gate's carry condition refuses **every** new senior share while that carry stands; and if L-1 exceeds v2's holdings its price goes to 0 and the holder's redeposit reverts (`ZeroPrice`). **The calendar exists to prevent this: v2 is funded (redeposit right after S2) before its first settle, and A is never before S1.**
- The old junior stays the cover until its holder claims; the claim and the v2 deposit are minutes apart by the same owner. The fee recipient flip is a clean cut: V2 accrues first, so every fee second up to phase C goes to the old junior, every second after goes to v2.

## What if there is a loss around the old settles (M2, corrected)

A **Felix loss booked after phase A** (Felix `lostAssets` grows) is not forgiven on v2: `lostBase` was fixed at A by design and `v2.syncSenior()` returns early while a loss is booked, so **it cannot re-baseline**. From phase C the gate then refuses every fresh account (its Felix condition mirrors v2's), which blocks D's precheck. **Only a settle of a v2 that holds funds clears it** (the settle bills the loss and rebases). The earlier advice "call `v2.syncSenior()`" was wrong. Consequences and what to do:
1. The exposure window is A to v2's funding: **a later A shortens it** (the latest A = B, Thu 19 Nov 19:27, leaves 3 days; Mon 16 Nov leaves about 6.8). It is the same lever as the margin table.
2. Before D check `gate.canReceiveShares(<fresh address>)` and `v2.coverInProgress()`. D's precheck tolerates up to `maxUnrecognised` (10 USDC) of unrecognised cover; a loss branch waits for `coverInProgress == 0`.
3. If the gate refuses after C and you need senior open sooner: the gate owner (`0x9EDf`) has instant partial relief after C: `setAllowed(account, true)` for a specific account and `setMaxUnrecognised(x)` (cap 1,000 USDC). Never allowlist a pooled holder (RUNBOOK 3a item 6). Removing the gate is another 3-day timelock.
4. A cover paid by the old junior at S1 or S2 (from its idle: 5 USDC today; the keeper's settle tick recalls Felix first, so up to about 81) is recorded as `Cover(owed, paid)`; read the settle's events and `senior.convertToAssets(1e18)` against before. If it paid in full, v2's baseline is re-taken by `v2.syncSenior()` once the price is back at or above v2's expectation and no Felix loss is booked.

## Regenerating the plans at the real nonce

CREATE addresses depend on the signer's nonce (`0x2DBE` was at 27 on 12 Nov; `0x9EDf` at 1). The plans must be generated just before signing, on a fork of the current head:

```
anvil --fork-url https://hyperliquid.drpc.org --port 8945 --host 127.0.0.1 --gas-limit 30000000   # loopback only
export SENIOR=0x3F7382bd8BFC6C548CAa2D7B4dfCe628B12A9759 OLD_JUNIOR=0x20D5F4f78858D257eA047EB5F47773E361697F2E OLD_ROUTER=0xC2d2C5e56798Bfd0EEc67EEC12ddC2b521b1aA37
python3 ops/make_migration.py out/ http://127.0.0.1:8945 --holder      # live-state mode; fork-only cheats: funding, impersonation, warps
```
It writes `s-old-settle.json`, `a-deploy.json`, `b-submit.json`, `c-execute.json`, `d-cutover.json`, `manifest.json`, and refuses any RPC that is not `http://127.0.0.1:<port>` on an anvil. Each plan carries the signer's nonce at generation; `replay_plan.py` refuses a plan whose nonce differs. `python3 ops/replay_plan.py out/manifest.json http://127.0.0.1:8945` replays them again.

**Regenerating ONE phase** (for example D, because `0x2DBE` sent another transaction and its nonce moved): `make_migration.py` always re-runs from the start, so for a single phase fork the **current live head**, make sure the fork already holds the earlier phases (their results are on chain by then), and run `make_plan.py` for that phase with the explicit environment:

```
export FORK_RPC=http://127.0.0.1:8945 DEPLOYER=<signer> SENIOR=… OLD_JUNIOR=… OLD_ROUTER=… OLD_SETTLE_DUE=1794774420 \
       AGENT=0xC772F0ea992236dA71Fa30b8899D71B680866956 TREASURY=0xba08A51bb1BB49E9840Eb55511E8880F95306A40 \
       JUNIOR=<v2 from FACTS 0a> GATE=<gate from FACTS 0a> ROUTER=<router v2 from FACTS 0a>
python3 ops/make_plan.py 06d_CutOver.s.sol "phase D: cut-over" out/d-cutover.json     # 06a/06b/06c likewise; DEPLOYER is the phase's signer
```
The phase's prechecks (read live state from the fork) must pass on the fork before the plan is used; the plan then carries the signer's current nonce.

**The signer page supports the new plan fields, and fails closed.** `web/public/ops/deploy.html` (with `plancheck.js` beside it) now understands `precheck`, the `gte`, `word`, `codehash` and `codeof`/`contains` checks, `waitBeforeSeconds` / `waitFromPlan` / `notBeforeCall`, `nonce`, `bigBlocks` and `needsBigBlock`. Before step 1 it runs a gate and keeps "sign step 1" disabled until every line passes: the connected account is the plan's deployer, its nonce equals the plan's (otherwise regenerate the plan), the earliest allowed start has passed (for a wait it asks for the tx hash of the previous phase's last transaction and shows the earliest time in chain time), and every precheck holds. A `gte` or `word` check is a real comparison and is never weakened to "non-zero". **Any field the page does not know (plan, step or check), a check it cannot evaluate, a plan whose signer differs from its deployer or a chainId other than 999 makes it refuse the whole plan and name the field.** Transaction building and signing are unchanged; the page only adds read-only calls (`eth_call`, `eth_getCode`, `eth_getBlockByNumber`, `eth_getTransactionCount`, `eth_getTransactionReceipt`) on the RPC it already used. The keeper's settle plan (`s-old-settle.json`) is refused by design: it is not signed by the partner. Offline test: `node web/ops-tests/plancheck.test.cjs`. **The `cast` commands (and the plan's `precheck` / `verify` read-backs by hand) remain the fallback** if the page is unavailable or refuses a plan.
